What is a validation?

A validation checks whether a business object is in a valid state. If a rule is violated, the transaction should not finalize.

Examples

End date cannot precede start date; a required business partner must be valid; amount cannot be negative; a closed document cannot be changed.

Good messages

Explain what is wrong and, when possible, how to fix it. Avoid generic messages such as Error occurred.

Architect’s perspective

Validations protect business invariants. Do not rely only on UI validation because other consumers may call the same service.
