Test at multiple levels

A robust RAP application needs more than a happy-path UI test.

Behavior tests

Verify create/update/delete, validations, determinations, actions and authorization outcomes.

Service tests

Verify that the service exposes the correct entities, fields, navigation and operations.

UI tests

Confirm that the Fiori experience matches the business process.

Architect’s perspective

Test the business contract, not only the screen.
