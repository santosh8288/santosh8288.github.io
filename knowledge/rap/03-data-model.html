Start with the business object

Model a meaningful business concept rather than simply mirroring database tables. Identify the root, child entities and transactional relationships.

Root and child entities

A root entity can own child entities through composition. Associations describe relationships where the related entity can have an independent lifecycle.

Example

A travel business object might contain Travel as the root, with Booking and Attachment children. The exact model should follow the business lifecycle.

Projection thinking

The interface/data model and consumption model do not always need to be identical. Projection views can expose only what a particular service needs.

Architect’s perspective

Model business semantics first. Avoid exposing persistence details simply because they exist in a table.
