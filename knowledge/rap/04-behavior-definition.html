Behavior Definition

Declaring transactional capabilities, rules and operations of a RAP business object.

What it does

A behavior definition is the transactional contract for the business object. It describes what consumers can create, change and execute.

Typical capabilities

CRUD operations, actions, validations, determinations, locking, authorization and feature control.

Example

A behavior definition can declare create, update and delete, mark fields read-only, define actions such as Approve, and attach validations or determinations to lifecycle events.

Strictness

Use the strictness level supported by the target ABAP release and follow current SAP guidance.

Architect’s perspective

A good behavior definition reads like a business contract. Important business rules should have explicit ownership.
