RAP Determinations

Automatically deriving or adjusting business data in response to transactional events.

What is a determination?

A determination derives or adjusts values because something happened in the business object lifecycle.

Examples

Set a default status, calculate a derived amount, populate a timestamp, or derive a dependent field after a key field changes.

Keep them predictable

A determination should have a clear trigger and deterministic result. Avoid hidden side effects that surprise consumers.

Architect’s perspective

Use determinations for derivation. If an operation is an explicit business command, consider an action instead.
