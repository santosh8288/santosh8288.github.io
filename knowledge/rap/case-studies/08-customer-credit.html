Customer Credit Review — Approval & Authorization

A credit-review case combines an approval action with authorization and a decision history.

Case studiesAll scenarios01 · Purchase Order Approval02 · Travel Management — Draft to Active03 · Supplier Invoice — Validation & Determination04 · Sales Order — Action & Status Control05 · Employee Onboarding — Authorization & Feature Control06 · Plant Maintenance Request — EML07 · Stock Transfer — Transaction & Validation08 · Customer Credit Review — Approval & Authorization09 · Project Expense Claim — Draft + Validation + Approval
Scenario: a credit analyst reviews a customer credit request. Submit creates a Pending Review state; an authorized reviewer can Approve or Reject. Rejection requires a reason and every decision should be auditable.

Behavior

action submit result [1] $self; action approve result [1] $self; action reject parameter ZD_RejectReason result [1] $self;

Decision rules

DRAFT → Submit → PENDING_REVIEW PENDING_REVIEW → Approve → APPROVED PENDING_REVIEW → Reject(reason) → REJECTED A rejected request may be resubmitted after correction, depending on the business rule.

Architectural consideration

Keep the decision history separate from the current status if auditability is required. The status answers 'where is it now?'; the history answers 'what happened and who decided?'.

Production note: The code is intentionally a teaching/reference skeleton. CDS entity names, database fields, message classes, authorization implementation, released APIs and syntax can vary by ABAP platform/SAP S/4HANA release. Activate and test against your target system before treating it as production code.
