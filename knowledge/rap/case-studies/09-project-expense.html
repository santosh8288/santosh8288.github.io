Project Expense Claim — Draft + Validation + Approval

This combines several RAP concepts in one realistic employee process: a user builds an expense claim as a draft, validations check amounts, and approval changes the business state.

Business flow

Draft → Prepare → Activate ↓ SUBMITTED ↓ APPROVED/REJECTED

Validation examples

Expense date cannot be in the future. Amount must be positive. Project must be valid. Receipt may be mandatory above a configured threshold.

Architectural consideration
