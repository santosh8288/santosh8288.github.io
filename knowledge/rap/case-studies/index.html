RAP through real business scenarios.

A practical case-study library: purchase-order approval, travel draft, validations, actions, authorization, EML, transaction processing and more. Each scenario connects business requirements to RAP design and ABAP implementation.

Scenarios

CASE 01

Purchase Order Approval

A realistic RAP scenario for a purchase order that moves through Submit → Pending Approval → Approved/Rejected. This case intentionally separates the RAP business object from the enterprise workflow engine.

Open case study →CASE 02

Travel Management — Draft to Active

A travel-request business object is ideal for demonstrating managed draft. The employee can enter dates, destination and cost, leave the application, return later, and finally activate the request.

Open case study →CASE 03

Supplier Invoice — Validation & Determination

A supplier invoice demonstrates two classic RAP rules: derive a value automatically and block activation when business data is invalid.

Open case study →CASE 04

Sales Order — Action & Status Control

A sales order is useful for explaining explicit business actions such as Submit, Confirm and Cancel rather than treating every business operation as a generic update.

Open case study →CASE 05

Employee Onboarding — Authorization & Feature Control

An employee onboarding request demonstrates the difference between authorization and feature control: who may operate on the object versus whether an operation is currently available because of the object's state.

Open case study →CASE 06

Plant Maintenance Request — EML

A maintenance request demonstrates EML for reading a RAP business object and performing a transactional modification through the behavior contract.

Open case study →CASE 07

Stock Transfer — Transaction & Validation

A stock-transfer request shows how a transactional business object can validate source/destination and quantity before allowing a transfer operation.

Open case study →CASE 08

Customer Credit Review — Approval & Authorization

A credit-review case combines an approval action with authorization and a decision history.

Open case study →CASE 09

Project Expense Claim — Draft + Validation + Approval

This combines several RAP concepts in one realistic employee process: a user builds an expense claim as a draft, validations check amounts, and approval changes the business state.
